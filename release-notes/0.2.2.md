# OctezDesk 0.2.2

- **Unstake from your baker.** When OctezDesk holds your baker's master key (test networks), ☰ → **Baker keys…** now has an **Unstake** section. Enter an amount in tez or choose **Everything**. OctezDesk asks you to confirm, and warns you if less than 6,000 ꜩ would stay staked, because the baker then loses its baking rights.
- **Withdraw unstaked tez.** Unstaked tez stay frozen for a few cycles, and the same screen shows how much. When they're released, a **Withdraw** button moves them to your spendable balance.

If an earlier 0.2 version is installed, OctezDesk updates to this one by itself.
